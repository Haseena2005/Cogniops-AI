POLICY CODE: POL-004
TITLE: Emergency Expedited Freight Expenditure Limits
CATEGORY: Logistics & Freight
RULES:
1. Emergency air freight authorizations are capped at $25,000 per single procurement event.
2. Expedited freight is only authorized for orders with contract penalties exceeding air freight premiums.
3. Cumulative monthly emergency shipping variances must not breach $75,000 across all warehouses.
4. Carbon offset requirements apply to all expedited cargo lanes.
