POLICY CODE: POL-006
TITLE: Regional Demand Stimulation & Ad-Spend Reallocation
CATEGORY: Marketing
RULES:
1. Marketing reallocation to depressed sales territories is capped at $40,000 per quarter.
2. In-region lead generation campaigns must demonstrate a projected payback ratio of at least 2.5x revenue lift.
3. Channel marketing subsidies require joint commitment from certified regional distribution partners.
4. Budget cannibalization from growing regions must not exceed 12% of donor region marketing capital.
