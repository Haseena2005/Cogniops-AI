POLICY CODE: POL-008
TITLE: Single-Source Vendor Mitigation & Resilience Mandate
CATEGORY: Strategic Sourcing
RULES:
1. No single supplier may account for more than 45% of critical bill-of-materials components without dual-sourcing contracts.
2. Secondary supplier qualification audits must be refreshed every 180 days.
3. Standby allocation agreements must guarantee 30% volume delivery within 10 calendar days of invocation.
4. Foreign currency hedging must accompany international component re-routing.
