POLICY CODE: POL-001
TITLE: Supplier Switching & Dual-Sourcing Governance
CATEGORY: Procurement & Supply Chain
RULES:
1. Switching tier-1 component suppliers requires VP Procurement sign-off if financial exposure exceeds $50,000.
2. Expedited onboarding of secondary vendors is approved when existing supplier delay exceeds 14 business days.
3. Secondary supplier quality rating must be at least 4.2 out of 5.0.
4. Expedited shipping surcharge multiplier is strictly capped at 1.5x standard logistics baseline cost.
