POLICY CODE: POL-002
TITLE: Maximum Promotional Discounting & Pricing Guardrails
CATEGORY: Sales & Commercial Operations
RULES:
1. Maximum standard sales discount cannot exceed 15.0% without CFO authorization.
2. Promotional discount campaigns aiming to reverse regional sales drops are limited to a 90-day execution window.
3. Price reductions must never cause product gross profit margin to drop below 28.0%.
4. Bundled discounting across product lines requires automated margin contribution validation.
