POLICY CODE: POL-007
TITLE: Enterprise Operational Risk Tolerance & Capital Protection
CATEGORY: Corporate Governance
RULES:
1. Any operational strategy with worst-case downside exceeding $100,000 requires Chief Risk Officer approval.
2. Expected utility calculations must incorporate a 95% Value-at-Risk (VaR) penalty parameter.
3. Autonomous recommendation pipelines must present at least one conservative fallback strategy.
4. Human-in-the-loop signoff is mandatory for all irreversible contractual commitments.
