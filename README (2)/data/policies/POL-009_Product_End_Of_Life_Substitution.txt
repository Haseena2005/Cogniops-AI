POLICY CODE: POL-009
TITLE: Component Substitution & Product Cross-Compatibility Guidelines
CATEGORY: Engineering & Manufacturing
RULES:
1. Substituting micro-controllers or sensors with alternative part numbers requires QA automated bench-test clearance.
2. Direct pin-compatible substitutions are permissible during stockouts if performance variance is under 3%.
3. Customer notification is mandatory if component revision changes exterior casing dimensions or firmware signatures.
4. Scrap allowances for replaced legacy inventory are capped at $15,000 per incident.
