POLICY CODE: POL-005
TITLE: Enterprise Account SLA Breach & Customer Retention Protocol
CATEGORY: Customer Success & Accounts
RULES:
1. Any enterprise customer with satisfaction below 3.5 or delayed orders must be assigned an Executive Sponsor within 48 hours.
2. Retention concession credits are authorized up to 10.0% of Annual Contract Value (ACV) to prevent churn.
3. Contract termination penalties may be waived if delay exceeds 20 business days due to supplier bottleneck.
4. Proactive SLA delivery credits must be credited before billing cycles close.
